# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients and hiring teams reviewing Hasnat Amir's engineering work. Both audiences are equally important. The contact section helps visitors discuss either a project or a role.

## Product Purpose

Present Hasnat's experience and selected work, then provide a clear way to start a conversation. The primary contact action is sending a message; direct email is a secondary alternative.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, technical, and direct. Preserve the existing typography, blue accent, light and dark themes, and original terminal-window sidebar logo.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized social cards, repeated groups of identical links, competing contact actions, inconsistent content widths, and custom cursors.

## Design Principles

- Give clients and hiring teams equal clarity in contact copy and inquiry choices.
- Keep persistent profile shortcuts in the sidebar and mobile menu.
- Use one aligned contact form rather than a separate social-card grid.
- Reserve strong emphasis for the primary action.
- Preserve readable text and clear keyboard focus on every viewport.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use visible field labels, native inputs, keyboard access, responsive stacking, and clear submission feedback. Preserve support for light and dark themes and reduced motion.
